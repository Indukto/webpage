Privacy Policy — Zoom Box Camera

Effective date: October 12, 2026
App: Zoom Box Camera (Android, API 29+) · source code
Developer: indukto — https://indukto.eu
Contact: info@indukto.eu

Summary: Zoom Box Camera is fully offline. It collects, transmits, shares, and sells no personal data. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting. The app declares no INTERNET permission, so Android blocks all network access.

Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store on servers, or have access to names, emails, identifiers, location data, usage analytics, diagnostics, or advertising data.

On-device processing only

  • Camera frames, photos (JPEG + optional DNG RAW), and settings stay on your device.
  • Film processing (3D LUTs, grain, vignette, double exposure) runs locally — nothing is uploaded.
  • Sharing happens only when you tap Share, via Android's system share sheet.

Permissions used

  • CAMERA — to take photos (required).
  • Storage / media access — to save photos and show the in-app gallery. No other files are accessed.
  • No microphone, contacts, phone, or location permissions. No GPS tags in photo EXIF.

Third parties

No third-party SDKs. No data shared with third parties. Google Play may independently process install statistics — see Google Play's privacy policy.

Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Your rights (GDPR / EU)

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing to access or delete on our servers. Emails to info@indukto.eu are used only to handle your request. You may complain to your local data protection authority.

Security & retention

Photos stay under your control — delete them in-app or in the system gallery any time. Uninstalling removes the app's private data and settings.

Changes

If the app changes in a privacy-relevant way, this page and the in-app copy (Settings → Legal) will be updated. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

Contact

Privacy questions: info@indukto.eu
