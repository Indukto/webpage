Frequently Asked Questions

// 04 — faq & answers

Everything you need to know about using ZoomBox Camera, our retro film looks, custom 3D LUT importing, and offline privacy standards.

What makes ZoomBox Camera the best retro film camera app for Android? +

Unlike standard camera apps that only slap a generic filter on top of an already compressed JPEG, ZoomBox Camera reconstructs the complete shooting experience of an analog camera app.

It integrates calibrated 3D LUT color grading directly into your viewfinder via OpenGL fragment shaders, provides a signature rangefinder-style Zoom Box crop overlay, supports parallel uncompressed DNG RAW capture, and functions 100% offline with zero advertisements or tracking.

How does the signature Zoom Box viewfinder feature work? +

Digital zoom on smartphones usually crops into your image blindly after you take the shot. With ZoomBox Camera, the Zoom Box is a live framing border drawn across your viewfinder screen.

As you adjust zoom between 1× and 5×, the box dynamically scales so you see both the surrounding context and your precise crop area before pressing the shutter. You can also lock aspect ratios to 4:3, 3:2 (standard 35mm film), 16:9, or 1:1 square.

What vintage film looks and CCD digicam effects are included? +

ZoomBox Camera features 13+ hand-tuned presets:

  • CCD Digicam: Recreates the nostalgic color science, mild contrast clipping, and saturated flash look of early 2000s compact digital point-and-shoot cameras.
  • Warm Portrait: Flattering pastel warmth with soft skin-tone roll-off.
  • Golden 200: Sun-drenched amber tones reminiscent of classic 200 ISO color negative film.
  • Monochrome 400 & Street Mono: Classic high-contrast black-and-white street emulsions with organic silver grain.
  • Cross Process & Instant Vintage: Funky chemical shifts and nostalgic instant-film fading with subtle light leaks.
Can I import my own 3D LUTs or create custom vintage film presets? +

Yes! ZoomBox Camera includes an onboard Look Editor. You can create a preset from scratch, adjust tone curves, split-toning, grain intensity, halation bloom, and vignette sliders with immediate live feedback on the camera viewfinder.

Furthermore, you can import standard Adobe .cube 3D LUT files directly from your device storage, allowing you to use your favorite cinema or darkroom color profiles on your phone.

Does ZoomBox Camera capture uncompressed RAW (DNG) files? +

Yes. If your Android phone's camera sensor supports it via the Android Camera2 API, ZoomBox Camera captures full uncompressed DNG RAW files in parallel with the vintage film JPEG. You get an instantly shareable retro film print plus complete digital sensor data for advanced color grading in desktop software like Adobe Lightroom.

Is ZoomBox Camera really 100% offline and private? +

Absolutely. ZoomBox Camera does not declare the Android INTERNET permission. That means the Android OS physically forbids the application from establishing network connections.

There are no accounts to create, no analytics SDKs, no third-party advertisers, and no background tracking. Your photographs are saved directly to your phone's local storage and never leave your control. Furthermore, no GPS location coordinates are injected into photo EXIF data.

Which Android versions and devices are compatible? +

ZoomBox Camera is engineered with modern Android architecture (Jetpack Compose, CameraX, and Material 3) and supports all Android smartphones running Android 10 (API level 29) or newer.

Is ZoomBox Camera free on Google Play? +

Yes! ZoomBox Camera is completely free to install from Google Play. There are no paywalls for essential camera controls, no subscription fees, and no ad popups interrupting your shooting flow.
