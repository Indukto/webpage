## Overview

**ZoomBox Camera** is an innovative **retro film camera app** designed for Android photographers who love the organic soul of analog photography and vintage CCD digicams.

Unlike ordinary film filter apps that paste a flat color tint over a photo after capture, ZoomBox Camera renders real-time 3D Look-Up Tables (LUTs) in the viewfinder using an OpenGL ES fragment shader pipeline. Coupled with its signature **Zoom Box** framing overlay, full manual exposure control, custom LUT import, double exposure mode, and parallel **DNG RAW** capture, ZoomBox puts the darkroom back in your pocket.

## Key Features

### 1. The Signature Zoom Box Viewfinder
- **Live 1×–5× Zoom Box:** A dynamic rangefinder-style framing crop overlay shows you the exact crop boundary before you shoot.
- **Multiple Aspect Ratios:** Frame in classic 4:3, 3:2 (standard 35mm film), 16:9 widescreen, or 1:1 square.
- **Multi-Lens Support:** Switch effortlessly between ultra-wide, primary, and telephoto lenses, taking a center crop from full sensor resolution.

### 2. 13 Vintage Film Looks & CCD Digicam Effects
- **CCD Digicam:** Authentic early 2000s compact digital camera look with punchy flash response, slightly cool greens, and nostalgic Y2K sensor tones.
- **Warm Portrait:** Soft, flattering skin tones and gentle pastel highlight rolloff.
- **Monochrome 400 & Street Mono:** Gritty black & white emulsions with sharp contrast and rich silver-halide grain.
- **Golden 200:** Warm 200-speed consumer color negative film emulation with amber shadows.
- **Instant Classic & Instant Vintage:** Clean and aged instant-print aesthetics with light leak artifacts.
- **Cross Process:** Dramatic chemical shifts, high contrast, and vivid yellow-green hues.
- **Procedural Film Artifacts:** Filmic shoulder roll-off, halation bloom, black-point fade, organic grain, dust specks, vertical scratches, and warm corner light leaks.

### 3. Creative Photography Tools & Look Studio
- **Custom Look Editor:** Build your own custom film look. Fine-tune tone curves, split-toning, grain intensity, bloom, and vignetting with real-time viewfinder feedback.
- **Custom 3D LUT (.cube) Import:** Import industry-standard Adobe `.cube` files directly from your phone's storage.
- **Double Exposure Mode:** Creative screen-blending that fuses consecutive shots into a single atmospheric composition.
- **Parallel RAW (DNG) Capture:** Record full-resolution uncompressed 14-bit RAW alongside graded film JPEGs via Android Camera2.
- **In-App Photo Editor & Gallery:** Review, adjust, and export your retro shots without leaving the app.

### 4. Pro Manual Controls
- **Exposure Compensation:** Dial in −3 to +3 EV in precise 0.1 EV increments.
- **2D Color Temperature & Tint Plot:** Visually balance warm golden hour tones or cool cyan shadows.
- **Hardware Trigger:** Volume-key shutter support and 3s, 5s, or 10s self-timer.

### 5. Private by Design & 100% Offline
- **No Internet Permission:** The app does not request `INTERNET` access in its Android manifest, making remote tracking technically impossible.
- **Zero Ads, Zero Telemetry:** No analytics SDKs, no ad networks, no cloud uploads.
- **Local Storage Only:** Photos stay securely on your phone. No GPS location coordinates are written into EXIF tags.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is a retro film camera app and how does ZoomBox Camera work?
A retro film camera app recreates the colors, contrast curves, optical imperfections, and grain of vintage 35mm film and nostalgic CCD digital cameras. ZoomBox Camera uses an advanced OpenGL ES shader pipeline that applies real 3D LUTs in real time directly inside the live camera viewfinder.

### How does the signature Zoom Box viewfinder feature work?
The Zoom Box is a live crop-box overlay on the viewfinder that scales across 1× to 5× zoom. Instead of guessing how digital zoom will frame your picture, the Zoom Box outlines the exact sensor crop area (supporting 4:3, 3:2, 16:9, and 1:1 aspect ratios) before you press the shutter button.

### Can I import my own 3D LUTs or create custom presets?
Yes. ZoomBox Camera includes an onboard Look Editor allowing you to adjust tone curves, split-toning, grain intensity, halation bloom, and vignetting with live viewfinder feedback. You can also import external Adobe `.cube` 3D LUT files directly from your phone's storage.

### Does ZoomBox Camera support RAW (DNG) photography?
Yes. On supported Android devices, ZoomBox Camera captures full uncompressed DNG RAW sensor data in parallel with the vintage film JPEG, giving you an authentic film print plus raw files for desktop color grading in Adobe Lightroom.

### Is ZoomBox Camera really 100% private and offline?
Yes. ZoomBox Camera does not declare the `INTERNET` permission in its Android manifest. Android physically prevents the app from accessing the internet. There are zero ads, zero trackers, zero analytics, and zero cloud uploads. Photos remain strictly on your device.

### Which Android devices and versions are supported?
ZoomBox Camera is built with Jetpack Compose, CameraX, and Material 3, and supports any Android smartphone running Android 10 (API level 29) or newer.

### Is ZoomBox Camera free on Google Play?
Yes, ZoomBox Camera is completely free to download from Google Play with no subscription fees and no in-app advertising.
