# Privacy Policy — Zoom Box Camera

English · Deutsch · Français · Español · Italiano · Polski

**Effective date:** October 12, 2026
**App:** Zoom Box Camera (Android, API 29+)
**Developer:** indukto — https://indukto.eu
**Contact:** info@indukto.eu

## Data we collect

None. We do not collect, store on servers, or have access to:

- names, emails, identifiers, or account data
- location data
- usage analytics or diagnostics
- advertising data

The app declares no `INTERNET` permission, so Android blocks all network access. No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting.

## On-device processing only

- Camera frames, photos (JPEG + optional DNG RAW), and app settings stay **on your device**.
- Film processing (3D LUTs, grain, vignette, double exposure) runs locally — nothing is uploaded.
- Sharing a photo happens only when **you** tap Share, using Android's system share sheet.

## Permissions used

- `CAMERA` — to take photos. Required.
- Storage / media access (`READ_MEDIA_IMAGES` or legacy storage on older Android) — to save photos and show the in-app gallery. No other files are accessed.
- No microphone, contacts, phone, or location permissions are requested.
- No GPS / location tags are written into photo EXIF.

## Third parties

No third-party SDKs. No data is shared with third parties. Google Play may independently process install/download statistics — see [Google Play's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

## Your rights (GDPR / EU)

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete on our servers. If you contact us at info@indukto.eu we reply only to handle your request and delete the email afterwards. You may also lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

## Security & retention

Your photos remain under your control — delete them in the in-app gallery or your system gallery at any time. Uninstalling the app removes its private data and settings.

## Changes

If the app ever changes in a privacy-relevant way, this policy at [https://indukto.eu/privacy.html](https://indukto.eu/privacy.html) will be updated and the in-app copy (Settings → Legal) will match. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

## Contact

Privacy questions: **info@indukto.eu**
